# Design your header and footer

Click **Design pages** in the preview panel. Pick a page style, then click any spot of the page's top or bottom edge to put text, a logo, the page number, the date or the document title there.

Every choice shows on the real PDF at once. Press **Save** to keep the design: it goes to one settings file that the command line and your other editors share.
