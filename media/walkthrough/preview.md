# See the real PDF while you write

Open a Markdown file and use the preview button at the top right of the editor.

The panel shows the PDF itself, with its header, footer and page numbers. It is drawn again a moment after you stop typing, saved or not, and it follows the Markdown file you bring to the front.

Raw HTML that was left out for safety, and diagrams that could not be drawn, appear in the Problems panel on the line that caused them.

[Open the PDF preview](command:markdownPdfExport.openPreview)
